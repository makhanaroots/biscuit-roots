BISCUIT ROOTS — E-COMMERCE WEBSITE PROJECT

Website pages (6 total):
1. index.html — Home + About Us
2. foxnuts.html — Product Category 1: Classic Biscuits
3. flavoured.html — Product Category 2: Chocolate & Flavours
4. premium.html — Product Category 3: Cookies & Premium
5. gift-boxes.html — Product Category 4: Biscuit Gift Boxes
6. contact.html — Contact details + enquiry form

HOW TO PREVIEW
- Extract the ZIP file.
- Open index.html in Chrome or another browser.
- Internet is required for the remote photos and Google Fonts.

HOW TO PUBLISH FREE WITH NETLIFY
1. Extract this ZIP first.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop in your browser and sign in if asked.
3. Drag the extracted folder named biscuit-roots-site (the folder containing index.html) onto the Netlify Drop area.
4. Wait for deployment. Netlify will provide a public website URL. Open it and test all six pages.

BEFORE SUBMISSION
- This is a student-project demo, not a fully integrated store. Prices and product details are illustrative.
- Replace phone and address placeholders on contact.html with accurate details.
- Social links currently point to generic Instagram and Facebook pages; replace them with brand profiles.
- Contact and checkout enquiries are sent through the visitor's email app to sachinakaha@gmail.com.
- The cart works in the browser, but no payment gateway, inventory, or order database is connected.
- Product photos are remote Unsplash images and require internet access.

GOOGLE ANALYTICS (OPTIONAL)
Create a Google Analytics 4 property and web data stream, then add Google's official tag using your own Measurement ID (starts with G-) to the <head> of all six HTML pages. Analytics won't report visits until a valid ID is installed and the deployed site receives traffic. Add a privacy notice before collecting analytics data.
